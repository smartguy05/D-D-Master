# DM tool catalog

The Realtime DM calls these tools, and the host can call them through `POST /api/tools/:name`.
The schemas are in `packages/shared/src/tools.ts`. Ids can be replaced by names, which are resolved
by exact match, then player name, then prefix.

| Tool | Args | Effect / returns |
|---|---|---|
| `get_party_status` | — | Location, grid, characters (HP, AC, conditions, dice mode, gold, items, pos, plus `subclass`, `slots` "1:2/4 2:0/3" (left/max), `pact` "L3 1/2", `hitDice` "4/5 d6", `concentration`, `deathSaves` while at 0 HP, `resources` ["Rage 2/3"]), monsters (HP, AC, attacks, pos), combat order, `map_objects` at the current location (id, kind, name, pos, items, gold, hidden/used only when true; omitted when none) |
| `roll_dice` | `notation, label, roller_id?, secret?, dc?, target_id?, attack_name?, action_type?, override?` | Rolls; the TV animates it. With `target_id` (attack roll, not secret) the TV also animates the attack from the roller to the target (see automatic effects). Returns total, dice (dropped in parentheses), modifier, natural 20 or 1, success. A `roller_id` matching no creature (removed, misspelled) returns `{error: "Unknown roller …"}` and rolls nothing. A d20 roll with `target_id` (another creature; a label with "save", "check" or "contest" is not an attack) and no `dc` is checked against the target's AC (`dc` = AC, output `vs_ac`); an explicit `dc` wins. Attack rolls follow the SRD: a natural 20 always hits, a natural 1 always misses. A secret attack keeps `dc`/`success` off the table and returns `hit` to the DM only. **Walls**: an attack roll with a `roller_id` where both creatures have tokens on the board (x, y ≥ 0) gets SRD cover from `coverBetween` (los.ts; other tokens and `blocks` features are blockers that give at most half cover). Total cover (no line of sight) returns `{error: "<Roller> has no line of sight to <Target>: a wall or closed door blocks it. …"}` and rolls nothing, even with an explicit `dc`. With no `dc`, half / three-quarters cover raise `vs_ac` by 2 / 5 and the output has `cover` and `cover_bonus` (the stored roll keeps `cover` for the breakdown). No tokens (theatre of mind): no check. In combat a d20 roll with `target_id` spends the attacker's action (or `action_type` bonus_action/reaction); refused past the [turn budget](../gameplay/turn-economy.md) |
| `request_player_roll` | `character_id, notation, label, dc?, target_id?, attack_name?, kind?, critical?, action_type?, override?` | Virtual: rolls now (an attack with `target_id` is animated). `kind: "damage"` (with `target_id`) is the damage roll after a hit: it spends nothing; virtual dice roll it at once (no attack animation; a `hint` says to `apply_damage`), physical dice set `pendingRoll` kind `damage` (a `replaced` note when it displaces another player's roll) and recording the total applies it to the target. A targeted d20 roll with no `dc` uses the target's AC (`vs_ac`), also stored on the pending roll. Attacks (not `kind: "damage"`, saves or checks) get the same line-of-sight and cover rules as `roll_dice`: total cover returns `{error}` and sets no pending roll; cover raises `vs_ac` (and the physical pending `dc`, with `pendingRoll.cover`). Physical: sets `pendingRoll` (kind `check`, with `targetId`/`attackName`) and returns an instruction; `replaced` warns when it overwrote a pending death or concentration save. Attacks spend the turn budget like `roll_dice` |
| `record_physical_roll` | `character_id, notation, label, total, natural?, dc?, kind?, critical?` | Records a spoken physical result and clears that character's `pendingRoll`. `natural` (1–20, only for a notation with a d20) is stored as the kept d20 (`dice: [{sides:20,value}]`, `modifier = total − natural`), so a natural 20 crits the attack and the TV plays the crit/fumble flourish; the output echoes `natural`. `kind` (default: the pending roll's kind) `death_save` applies the d20 (1–20) as a death save (on that PC's own turn, if it leaves them unable to act the turn passes on: `passed`, `next_turn`, `next_death_save`, `instruction`), `concentration` keeps or ends concentration against `dc`, `damage` (a pending damage roll) applies the total (negative counts as 0) to its target like `apply_damage` (`critical`, else the pending roll's `critical`: set by `request_player_roll critical` or inferred from a natural 20 attack just before; the pads have a toggle) and returns `{damage_applied, target, …hp}`. A check recorded with `kind: "check"` while that character's damage roll waits keeps the damage prompt, with a `warning`. `kind: "check"` while that character has a pending death or concentration save is applied as the pending save, with a `warning`. A pending check with a `targetId` animates the attack (outcome from `dc`). A DM call with no pending roll within 15 s after the phone pad or host recorded that character's physical roll (`RollResult.source` `host`), whatever total the DM heard, returns `already_recorded: true` with `recorded_total` and changes nothing; a new roll needs `request_player_roll` first |
| `apply_damage` | `target_id, amount, damage_type?, critical?, source_id?, attack_name?` | Temp HP first, clamped at 0. Foe at 0: Dead; an ally (`ally: true`) is knocked out (Unconscious) and stays on the board until healed or removed. PC: the [SRD 0-HP rules](../gameplay/rules-engine.md#death-saving-throws) (Unconscious, massive damage, failures at 0 HP, 2 on `critical`) and a concentration save. Returns HP plus `down`, `dead`/`cause`, `death_saves`, `concentration`, `concentration_ended`. A PC's death plays a `death` moment; a foe taken from >0 to 0 HP plays `death` with outcome `monster`. A foe dropped while others of the combat still stand adds `foes_standing: ["Name (id, hp/max HP)"]` (hidden ones too) so the DM doesn't end the fight early |
| `heal` | `target_id, amount` | Clamped at max HP; clears Unconscious; resets death saves and clears a pending death save. A PC brought up from 0 HP plays a `revived` moment with outcome `rallied`. A Dead PC: `{error}` pointing to `revive`, nothing changes |
| `set_temp_hp` | `target_id, amount` | Keeps the higher of the old and new values (5e rule) |
| `add_condition` / `remove_condition` | `target_id, condition`; add: `source_spell?, caster_id?, duration_rounds?, until_end_of_turn_of?` | Title-cased, no duplicates. Incapacitated, Unconscious, Paralyzed, Petrified or Stunned ends a PC's concentration (`concentration_ended`). With a source or duration, `conditionMeta` records why: linked to the caster's concentration on `source_spell` (automatic when concentrating on it), timed by rounds (start of the caster's, else the target's, turn) or until the end of a creature's turn (its next turn when given on its own turn) ([rules engine](../gameplay/rules-engine.md#spell-conditions)). `remove_condition` drops the meta too |
| `give_item` | `character_id, name, qty=1, description?` | Stacks by case-insensitive name |
| `remove_item` | `character_id, name, qty=1` | Exact or partial name; removes the entry at 0 |
| `adjust_gold` | `character_id, amount` (whole gp) | Negative spends; errors if it can't be afforded, and for a monster id ("only player characters have gold") |
| `split_gold` | `total, character_ids` | Shares a reward in whole gp: `floor(total / n)` each, the remainder 1 gp each to the first in the list (100 over 3: 34/33/33). Returns `{total, shares: [{name, amount, gold}]}` |
| `move_token` | `entity_id, x, y, ignore_terrain?, override?` | A DM move of a token already on the board walks the shortest route around walls, closed doors, windows and `blocks` features (`planMove` → `findPath`; 8 neighbours, diagonals one square) and returns `{name, pos, squares, around?}`. An occupied cell, or (DM) one under a `blocks` feature, goes to the nearest free reachable cell; an unreachable cell (a closed door, a sealed room) goes to the reachable cell closest to it. Both return `redirected: true, reason, note` (the note says to leave the token there, beside the feature when it is, or to describe where it stopped and open a door). `{error: "<Name> did not move and is still at (x, y): …"}` only when nothing reachable is closer. `ignore_terrain: true` is the DM's ruling (a jump, a climb, squeezing through, a wrong map): it goes straight there past walls and onto furniture, `terrain_ignored: true`. The host and a newly placed token are never checked. Clamped to the grid. The session refuses a third move of one token in one reply. In combat (DM calls) refused off-turn or past the movement left, counting the route's squares (5 ft each; the straight line with `ignore_terrain`); see [turn economy](../gameplay/turn-economy.md) |
| `spawn_monster` | `name, count=1, x?, y?, near?, formation=cluster\|arc\|line, fog=any\|revealed\|hidden, hidden=false, hp?, ac?` | SRD stats when the name matches; numbered names; sprites generated. Tokens are laid out by `planSpawn` around x/y, else the `near` feature (name: exact, prefix, whole word, contains; unknown name lists the features), else the focal feature, else right-middle: never on `blocks` features or other tokens, 3+ squares from the party when there is room (not when an exact x and y are given), preferring the `fog` side. `hidden=true` spawns them hidden from the TV (ambush) |
| `remove_monster` | `monster_id` | Removes the monster (or NPC), its token and its initiative entry. The current turn stays on the same creature (removing the current one passes the turn to the next; past the end of the order that starts a new round). A pending physical roll aimed at it is cancelled (`dropped_roll` in the result). When the current creature leaves, the next one's turn starts now: effects ending then wear off and a downed PC makes its death save (`next_turn`, `death_save`, `instruction` in the result) |
| `drop_loot` | `monster_id?, x?, y?, items=[{name, qty=1, description?}], gold=0, name?` | Puts a loot object (`state.mapObjects`, kind `loot`) on the current location: at `x,y` when both are given (they win over `monster_id`), else on the monster's token cell (a dead monster keeps its token until `remove_monster`, so call it first). Items get fresh ids and trimmed names; the name defaults to "<monster>'s loot" or "Loot". Errors (state unchanged): no `monster_id` and no `x,y`, unknown monster or one without a token (pass `x,y`), no current location, cell off the grid, nothing to drop. Returns `object_id, name, pos, items ("Dagger x2"), gold`; the service then draws each item's icon and the loot-sack marker in the background (`lootDropped` → `drawLootIcons`) |
| `pick_up` | `object_id, character_id, items?, gold?` | Moves items and gold from a map object at the current location into a player character's inventory (merged like `give_item`; an existing stack gains the item's icon) and gold. Omit `items` to take everything (gold too); with `items` (names, case-insensitive, whole stacks) gold is taken only with `gold: true`. Empty loot is removed; an interactable never is. No distance check. Errors (state unchanged, no toast): unknown object, another location, not a PC, a name the object doesn't hold (lists what it holds), nothing to take (no items named and no gold, e.g. a lever or `items: []` without `gold: true`). Returns `character, taken, gold, remaining {items, gold}, removed, inventory`; the TV gets a `pickup` event (toast with icons, not persisted) |
| `place_interactable` | `name, x, y, hidden=false` | Adds an interactable (lever, chest, altar…) on the current location's cell; the TV draws a pulsing aura unless `hidden`. Errors: no current location, cell off the grid. Returns `object_id, name, pos, hidden` |
| `set_interactable` | `id, used?, hidden?, name?` | Changes only the given fields of any map object (loot too): `used: true` dims the aura, `hidden` hides/reveals it on the TV. Unknown id errors. Returns the object summary |
| `remove_map_object` | `id` | Removes a loot pile or interactable. Unknown id errors |
| `set_disposition` | `monster_id, disposition: foe\|ally\|neutral, combatant?` | Changes a creature's side (`ally`/`neutral` flags; `combatant` when given, and `foe` makes it a combatant unless `combatant: false`). A knocked-out ally turned foe is Dead (foes at 0 HP are). Neutral creatures and non-combatants are taken out of a running initiative order; a creature that now fights and isn't in it rolls initiative and is slotted in (the current turn stays put). Returns `{name, disposition, combatant, joined_initiative?, in_combat?}`. The host's Monsters panel cycles it. It is the hard override and syncs the attitude: foe → hostile (score at most -3), neutral → indifferent (a stored score clamped into -2..2, else 0), ally → friendly (score at least +3) |
| `adjust_attitude` | `npc_id, delta (non-zero int -5..5), reason` | Moves a monster's/NPC's 2024 SRD attitude on a -5..+5 score (`attitudeOf`: stored `attitudeScore`/`attitude`, else derived from the flags: foe -3 Hostile, neutral 0 Indifferent, ally +3 Friendly). New score = clamp(score + delta, -5, 5), stored in `attitudeScore`, with `attitude` = `attitudeForScore` (<= -3 hostile, -2..+2 indifferent, >= +3 friendly). Crossing a threshold applies the same disposition logic as `set_disposition` (`applyDisposition`: hostile → foe and combatant, joining a running initiative order if alive; indifferent → neutral, leaving it and passing the turn on if it was its turn; friendly → ally, `combatant` unchanged), logs `<Name> is now Friendly (+3): <reason>` and plays an `attitude` moment (the token ring flashes). Unlike `set_disposition`, drifting into Hostile never kills a creature that is Unconscious at 0 HP; it stays down. A change refreshes the DM's "NPCs here" list (so do `set_disposition`, `add_npc`, `spawn_monster` and `remove_monster`). Within a band only the score moves (log line, no moment). Refuses PCs and unknown ids. Returns `{name, attitude, score, previous_attitude, previous_score, changed, disposition?, joined_initiative?, in_combat?}` |
| `add_npc` | `name, appearance?, stat_block?, follows?, ally?, combatant?, x?, y?, near?, voice? (≤200)` | Puts a named NPC on the map as a `Monster` with `npc: true`: stats from the SRD `stat_block` (default Commoner), its own portrait sprite drawn from its name + `appearance` (an outline NPC's description when omitted; outline names are matched case-insensitively). The token stands next to the party unless x/y/near are given. `follows` keeps it with the party through `change_scene` and map restaging (an ally knocked out at 0 HP is carried along); `ally` gives a green token and lists it with the party's allies. `combatant` puts it in `start_combat`'s initiative. A new NPC defaults to `follows: true, ally: true` and `combatant: false` for an ally (true for a hostile one, `ally: false`); one added with `combatant` during combat joins the initiative order at once. Calling it again for the same name updates only the `follows`/`ally`/`combatant` it is given (omitted ones stay; a non-combatant leaves a running initiative order), moves it when x/y/near are given, and redraws the sprite when the appearance changes. `voice` (direction for `speak_as_npc`) is stored in `Monster.voice`; without it an outline NPC takes the outline voice, else `defaultNpcVoice(name)` generates a deterministic one (no LLM; the same direction `speak_as_npc` falls back to, so the voice does not change once placed). A re-call with `voice` updates it. New NPCs get `attitude`/`attitudeScore` from their side (ally → friendly/+3, `ally: false` → hostile/-3); a re-call with `ally` syncs it |
| `start_combat` | — | Rolls initiative for everyone not dead (downed PCs too: they make death saves; hidden monsters included). Monsters with `combatant: false` (story NPCs from `add_npc`) and neutral creatures stay out; returns the order. If the first creature is a downed, unstable PC, its death save is made (`death_save`). Returns `announce`: the DM opens with one or two sentences that the battle has begun and the order (hidden ambushers not named) while the TV plays the intro. When a monster or NPC goes first, `instruction` tells the DM to take its whole turn after that announcement. Plays a `combat_start` moment (battle intro) and then a `turn_start` for the first creature |
| `next_turn` | — | Next combatant (skips dead monsters and dead PCs); round increments on wrap. Landing on a PC at 0 HP who is not Stable makes its death save (`death_save` in the result: rolled now for virtual dice, `pendingRoll` kind `death_save` for physical). On a monster or NPC turn the result has `instruction` (take the whole turn in this reply, then `next_turn`) and `ally` for allies. Conditions that end at the end of the finished turn or the start of the new one are removed (`wore_off`). A PC who can't take the turn they land on (Dead after the save, or still unconscious at 0 HP) passes it on at once: `passed: [{name, state, death_save}]`, and `instruction` tells the DM to say so and continue with the creature now named in `current` (also in `start_combat` and when `remove_monster` passes the turn). Plays a `turn_start` moment for the new creature (so does `remove_monster`/`set_disposition` when it passes the turn) |
| `end_combat` | `outcome?` (`victory`\|`fled`\|`defeat`) | Clears combat (and the turn budget, and `combat.announced`). Out of combat it does nothing: `{ok, note: "No combat is running."}`, no banner, no log line (a redundant second call shows no second victory). With `outcome` the TV plays that banner (a `moment`); omitted, it auto-detects: `victory` when this combat's foes (`combatFoes`) existed and none is standing, else `defeat` when every PC is at 0 HP or dead, else no banner. A victory/defeat already announced automatically this combat is not shown twice. A DM `outcome: victory` while a living `combatFoes` foe remains is refused with `{error}` naming them, and combat goes on (the host can force it; no outcome or `fled` still end it). Returns `{ok, outcome}`; the log says `Combat ends (victory).` |
| `end_campaign` | `outcome` (`success`\|`failure`), `epilogue?` | Service tool (not in `ENGINE_TOOLS`), callable by the DM and the host (`POST /api/tools/end_campaign`). Only when the campaign's story is over, never for one fight or one PC's death. A `failure` from the DM is refused (`{error}`, nothing changes) until the campaign's `deathRule` is met (`deathRuleMet`: every PC Dead for `party_wipe`, any PC Dead for `any_death`); the host can always call it. The service also calls it by itself (source `auto`) when an engine tool meets the death rule: see *Death rule* below. Sets `Campaign.status` to `completed`/`failed` and `completedAt`, saves and broadcasts the campaign, logs it, and plays the `campaign_end` moment with the epilogue on the TV. It refreshes the DM's instructions (they state a finished campaign) and, when the host called it, adds a silent `Host did: end_campaign…` system note for the live DM. Then saves every PC with a `rosterId` back to the global character roster (`saveToRoster`, a fresh snapshot of its end-of-campaign state; a failed save is skipped and does not fail the tool). Returns `{ok, status, outcome, roster: {saved: string[] /* names */, unlinked: {id, name}[]}}` (`unlinked` feeds the host's "Save all to roster" prompt); `{error}` when the campaign is not `active` (`resetCampaign` reopens it), and then nothing is saved |
| `take_action` | `entity_id?, kind, cost?, note?, override?` | Combat: spends the action (default), `bonus_action` or `reaction` on Dash (adds Speed to movement), Dodge, Disengage, Help, Hide, Ready, Search, Study, Influence, Magic, Utilize, other. Refused if already spent (`{error, refused: "turn_economy", budget}`); returns `budget`. Out of combat: a no-op note |
| `change_scene` | `location_id` (id or name), `forced?` | **Ready check** (`game/readyCheck.ts`, DM calls only): leaving the scene in focus with a human player's character is refused once with `{error, ask: [names]}` until a human player says something (an answered voice turn, counted even before its transcript arrives, or a typed line not from an AI seat), then a repeat to the same place goes through. The answer counts only within 5 minutes and 8 human lines of the ask (`READY_CHECK_MAX_AGE_MS`, `READY_CHECK_MAX_INPUTS`); later, the repeat is refused as a fresh check. A pending check is dropped when the party moves by any path (host move, split focus), on campaign load and on a new DM session (not a call handover). Skipped for the first arrival, AI-only movers, the host, an unknown location (the normal error), and `forced: true` (trapdoor, teleport trap). New map, monsters cleared except living followers (`follows`), party and followers placed at the entrance, instructions refreshed. On the first visit to a location with `fog: true` (default) fog of war turns on; returns `fog: boolean` |
| `change_scene` (split) | `location_id, character_ids?` | **Party split** ([party-split](../gameplay/party-split.md)): with `character_ids` only those PCs (plus listed companion NPC ids) go. They leave their origin's initiative, and an origin whose last PC left takes its followers along and is dropped. Their location becomes the scene in focus: a fresh one (empty combat, first-visit fog), or another group's, which they join (placed by the entrance, rolling into a running fight). It refuses PCs already there. Returns `arrived, here, joined_initiative?, left_behind?` (frozen fight), `other_groups?, instruction?`. Without ids while split, everyone regroups at the destination |
| `focus_scene` | `location_id` | Party split: cut to another group's scene. **Automatic effects** (no tool call needed; `apps/server/src/engine/effects.ts`):
- `apply_damage` with amount > 0 emits a `hit` (tinted by `damage_type`). With `source_id` it also
  carries the source and a travel `style` when the attack resolves (`attack_name`; else, with no
  `attack_name`, the source's only attack if it is not an SRD spell and its damage type matches
  `damage_type` when given); otherwise (breath weapons, Fireball follow-ups…), and for area spells (Fireball damage), it stays a plain hit with no
  travel. `critical` → outcome `crit`.
- `heal` emits `heal`.
- An attack roll (`roll_dice`, `request_player_roll`, or `record_physical_roll` of a pending check)
  with a known `target_id`, not secret, emits one `attack` effect: `style` melee/ranged/spell from
  the attacker's attack matched by `attack_name` or the label (monster attacks keep "Melee/Ranged
  Attack Roll" in `description`; an entry without that text named exactly like an SRD spell, e.g. a
  PC's "Fire Bolt", is that spell; a ranged attack with a non-physical damage type is a spell orb;
  "Melee or Ranged" is melee within 1 cell of the target, else ranged), else an SRD spell of that
  name, else weapon keywords (bow, crossbow, sling, dart, javelin, blowgun, net, thrown);
  `outcome` natural 20 → crit, natural 1 → miss (the kept die with advantage), else vs `dc` (none
  without a dc). Any visible roll that produced no attack effect (no or unknown target) with a
  natural 20 or 1 emits `crit` or `miss` on the roller.
- `cast_spell` (success only): spell attack → a travel-only `attack` orb per target (no outcome;
  the attack roll that follows brings hit/miss/crit); darts without an attack roll (Magic Missile)
  → orbs with outcome `hit`, `count` = darts split over the targets (≤ 6 each, never 0: with fewer
  darts than targets only the first targets get one); area → one `area` effect at `x, y`, else the first target, else
  the caster (emanations on the caster), `radius` = sphere/emanation/cylinder ft/5, cube and cone
  half their size, line half its width (a line with targets flies an orb to each instead); other
  damaging spells → an element burst (`fireball`, `frost`, `radiant`…) on each target; healing →
  `heal`; anything else → `arcane` on the targets or the caster. **Automatic reveal**: after every engine tool, when
fog is on, each character reveals the cells within its `lightRadius` (default 6) that its token has line of sight to (`visibleCells`: walls and closed doors block it), plus the glow of lit features (which stops at walls too). A host edit of a character's `lightRadius` (`updateCharacter`) reveals at once, without waiting for a move.
**Automatic rules** (engine/rules, see [rules engine](../gameplay/rules-engine.md)): PC damage
applies the SRD 0-HP and death-save rules and asks for a concentration save; `next_turn` makes a
downed PC's death save. Physical-dice players get a `pendingRoll` of kind `death_save` or
`concentration`, applied by `record_physical_roll`.

**Host-only tools** (`HOST_ONLY_TOOLS` in `packages/shared/src/tools.ts`: `edit_fog`,
`edit_map_feature`, `edit_walls`) are filtered out of the Realtime tool list (`dmToolDefs`) and `runTool` refuses
them when the source is the DM. The host's Board tab calls them through `POST /api/tools/:name`.

Adding a tool:
1. Add its schema and description in `ToolArgs` and `TOOL_DESCRIPTIONS`.
2. Add a handler in `engine/tools.ts` (list it in `ENGINE_TOOLS`), a rules handler in
   `engine/rules/*.ts` (listed in `RULES_TOOLS`/`RULES_HANDLERS` in `engine/rules/index.ts`), or a
   case in `GameService.runTool`.
3. Add a test and update this table.
4. If the DM should use it proactively, mention it in the persona in `realtime/instructions.ts`.

## Table moments and `victory_hint`

Engine tools collect transient `moments` (`EngineOutcome.moments`, `engine/moments.ts`) that
`runTool` broadcasts as `moment` events for the TV; they are never stored. After every engine tool
except `start_combat`/`end_combat`, `executeEngineTool` checks an active combat: when foes existed
(`combatFoes`: `foesOf` — not allies, not neutral — minus corpses outside the initiative order, so
the dead of an earlier fight in the scene never count) and every one is at 0 HP it plays `victory`
once per combat (recorded in `combat.announced`) and adds a string `victory_hint` to the tool output
telling the DM to narrate and call `end_combat` with outcome `victory`. While a PC is dying (0 HP,
not Stable, not Dead) the hint instead names them and says to keep the turns going until they are
healed or stabilised, then end the fight (death saves only roll at turn start); when every PC is at 0 HP or dead it plays
`defeat` once. Host HP edits (character patch) and `level_up` play no moment.

## Death rule (automatic campaign failure)

`Campaign.deathRule` (`party_wipe` default \| `any_death`) decides when character deaths end the
campaign. After every engine tool, `GameService.runTool` checks `deathRuleEnds(rule, before, after)`
(`engine/moments.ts`) while the campaign is `active`:
- `party_wipe`: every PC has the `Dead` condition after the tool and did not before. Unconscious or
  Stable PCs don't count, and a dead PC can be revived until the last one falls.
- `any_death`: a PC gained `Dead` in this tool.

When it fires, the service runs `end_campaign {outcome: failure}` (source `auto`: the `death` moment
plays first, then `campaign_end`), adds `campaign_ended: "failure: …"` to the tool's output, and
sends the live DM a system note. Host character edits (`PATCH /api/characters/:id`) don't trigger it;
the host has the End campaign button. Set the rule on the Adventure tab (`PATCH /api/campaign/settings`).
